Questions

Is Tahanan free for renters?

Yes. Making an account, saving places and messaging owners costs nothing.

Why do I need a face scan and an ID?

It keeps fake accounts out. Owners know a real person is asking about their room, and you know the owner was checked too. Only the Tahanan team sees your scan and ID.

Do I pay through Tahanan?

No. Pay the owner directly, only after you visit, and ask for a receipt and written terms.

How do I list my boarding house?

Create an owner account, finish the face scan and host profile, then upload a valid ID and business permit. We review owner accounts within 24 hours.

Safety guide

A safer search for your next home.

A few reminders for renters, students, and families looking for a place in Iligan City.

👀 Visit before paying

Whenever possible, visit the property in person and check the room, shared spaces, and surroundings.

🪪 Verify the owner

Ask for identification and confirm that the person is authorized to rent the property. Look for the ✓ Verified badge.

🧾 Keep records

Request written rental terms and receipts. Understand deposits, advance rent, utilities, and refund rules.

🚩 Watch for red flags

Be cautious of pressure to pay immediately, prices that seem too good to be true, or refusal to show the property.
