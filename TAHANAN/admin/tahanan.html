TAHANAN SAFETY GUIDE

A safer search for your next home.

A few reminders for renters, students, and families looking for a place in Iligan City.

👀 Visit before paying

Whenever possible, visit the property in person and check the room, shared spaces, and surroundings.

🪪 Verify the owner

Ask for identification and confirm that the person is authorized to rent the property. Look for the ✓ Verified badge.

🧾 Keep records

Request written rental terms and receipts. Understand deposits, advance rent, utilities, and refund rules.

🚩 Watch for red flags

Be cautious of pressure to pay immediately, prices that seem too good to be true, or refusal to show the property.
